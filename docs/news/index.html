Field report Market road stage, central Kenya 28 JUL 2026

What a boda stage already knows

Spend a morning at a working stage and the maintenance culture that keeps thousands of machines running becomes visible.

Riders who cover two hundred kilometres a day on the same machine develop diagnostic habits that no manual teaches. We spent a week listening rather than explaining.

Method
Observation and interview
Duration
Six mornings
Machines
Predominantly 125 to 150 cc
Daily distance
120 to 220 km reported
Sample
One stage, not representative
Status
Ongoing
