M.2How this market worksUsed and secondary

No checkout here. Every listing below is second hand and sold by its owner, not by Frontier Moto. Prices are set by sellers. Frontier notes condition and inspection honestly and takes no commission.

The standard

  • +Used on a route or inspected in person before it is listed
  • +Suited to heat, dust, load and poor fuel before it is suited to a catalogue
  • +Repairable where possible, and marked honestly where it is not
  • +Priced against what it replaces, not what it promises

What a fair price looks like

A fair price follows condition, history and parts availability, in that order. Condition is what the machine is today. History is what was done, by whom, and how long ago in kilometres. Parts availability is the risk most buyers underweight: a sound machine with a six week parts lead time is a different purchase from the same machine with parts on the shelf.
