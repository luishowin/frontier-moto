02.3Condition, not packagingHow equipment is judged here

A helmet is the one item on this page where used is the wrong economy. It absorbs an impact once, the damage that matters is inside the shell where you cannot see it, and a resale price tells you nothing about whether it has already done its job. Everything else in a loadout can be bought second hand and judged on wear.
